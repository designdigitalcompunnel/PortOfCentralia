About the park

Part of the Port’s industrial portfolio.

Industrial Park I is part of the Port of Centralia’s industrial property portfolio. The Port supports industrial development, business investment, and opportunities that contribute to the regional economy.

This page helps prospective businesses, developers, and other visitors understand the park and find the right next step.
